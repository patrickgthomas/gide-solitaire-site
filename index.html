Features

What it does.

♠

Big, clear cards

High-contrast cards in a typeface made for easy reading, sized to fill your phone or tablet.

✓

Tap to move

Tap a card and it goes to the best spot. No dragging, and a gentle message when a card has nowhere to go.

★

Every deal can be won

Each game is checked before it's dealt, so there's always a way to finish. Undo as often as you like.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can every game really be won?

Yes. Before a deal is shown, Gide Solitaire plays it out with every card visible and only keeps deals it can win. You can still get stuck by your own moves, and Undo always lets you back up and try another way.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in, and nothing leaves your device.

Can I make the suits easier to tell apart?

Yes. Turn on Four-color suits in Settings for blue diamonds and green clubs.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. The cards grow to fill the screen, in portrait or landscape.

How do I get support?

Email us at support@gidehub.com.
